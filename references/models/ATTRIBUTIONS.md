# Imported 3D models

These candidates were downloaded and evaluated. They are retained as references and are not loaded by the performance-focused scene or copied into the production build.

## Rock 07

- Creator: Jenelle van Heerden / Poly Haven
- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07
- License: CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license
- Download: glTF with 1K diffuse, normal, and AO/roughness/metalness maps.
- Changes: scaled to existing collision bounds; desaturated in the material to grey.

## Barramundi Fish

- Creator: Microsoft (2017)
- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/BarramundiFish
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: normalized scale and orientation; runtime swimming deformation and caustic lighting added. The original model is static and has no animation rig.

The other creatures and plants are project geometry with project-generated texture maps.
